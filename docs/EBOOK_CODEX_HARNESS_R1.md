## 3. Allowed

- reader/TTS/AI QA source/test/build;
- public-domain/synthetic fixtures;
- bounded adapter/migration/test engineering;
- offline/local engine diagnostics;
- source/test/build receipts.

## 4. Forbidden

- Self-hosted Runner registration;
- ambient page/playback/microphone/location/device capture;
- full copyrighted/private book context;
- Harness/Agent as reading knowledge truth;
- automatic KnowMe contribution;
- Story Atlas/3D without a named task;
- source repair during deployment;
- silent model/provider/network fallback;
- merge/release.
